# Antennas & Propagation

This repository contains the assignments for the **Antennas & Propagation** course at ECE AUTH during the 2024–2025 academic year.

## 📋 Contents

### 📁 Project A (Set 1): Antenna Arrays & Mutual Impedance
* **1.1 Planar Array**: Vertical and 3D radiation patterns of a $24 \times 16$ isotropic array for several steering directions $(\theta_0, \phi_0)$, its directivity found analytically and numerically (Riemann sum), and a redesign as a Hansen-Woodyard end-fire array.
* **1.2 Dipole with Plane Reflector**: Far field of a tilted $\lambda/2$ dipole in front of an infinite reflector using image theory, its radiation patterns, and the tilt that maximizes radiation in the horizontal plane.
* **1.3 Non-Uniform Linear Array**: Sidelobe level of a 10-element broadside array with a symmetric current distribution, first with linear interpolation and then with adjusted currents.
* **1.4 Mutual Impedance & Parasitic Arrays**: Mutual impedance of two parallel dipoles with the induced EMF method, then the input impedance, horizontal pattern and $|\Gamma|$ of a three-dipole parasitic array, with and without a plane reflector.

### 📁 Project B (Set 2): Numerical Analysis of Wire Antennas with NEC
* **2.1 Double Discone Antenna**: Input impedance, $|\Gamma|$ and SWR from $0.5f_0$ to $4f_0$, matching with $Z_0 = 180\ \Omega$, and radiation patterns at $f_0$, $2f_0$, $3f_0$ and $4f_0$.
* **2.2 Helical Antenna**: An axial-mode helix over a disk ground plane, its input impedance, the choice of $Z_0$ and its radiation patterns across the band.
* **2.3 Folded Dipole & Beverage Antenna**: Input impedance, resonance and radiation patterns of a folded dipole for three conductor spacings, and of a $5\lambda$ Beverage antenna over a perfect and over a real ground.
